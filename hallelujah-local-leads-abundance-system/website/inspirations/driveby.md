## Component Recipe

| # | Component | Section | Notes |
|---|-----------|---------|-------|
| 1 | `sticky-blur-nav` | Global | Transparent → solid on scroll |
| 2 | `video-hero` | Hero | SVG feColorMatrix indigo tint on tech stock video |
| 3 | `dot-rhombus` | Hero or Services | Canvas 2D diamond dot grid background |
| 4 | `marquee-ticker` | Between sections | Services/capabilities scrolling strip |
| 5 | `particle-torus` | Feature section | Three.js 8000-particle rotating knot as section background |
| 6 | `glassmorphism` | Services | blur + semi-transparent service cards |
| 7 | `scroll-reveal` | All sections | Staggered fade-in-up |
| 8 | `monospace-labels` | Throughout | Category labels |
| 9 | `gradient-text` | Headlines | Indigo → cyan gradient on key headlines |
| 10 | `ghost-outline` | CTAs | Transparent border CTA buttons |

## Page Structure (top to bottom)

1. **Navigation** — `sticky-blur-nav`
2. **Video Hero** — `video-hero` (indigo feColorMatrix tint)
3. **Marquee Strip** — `marquee-ticker` (services/capabilities)
4. **Services** — `dot-rhombus` background + `glassmorphism` cards + `scroll-reveal`
5. **Particle Torus Section** — `particle-torus` (Three.js 8000-particle rotating knot)
6. **Stats** — `gradient-text` headlines + `monospace-labels`
7. **CTA** — `ghost-outline` buttons
8. **Footer**

## What Makes This One Special

- **Heaviest visual effects stack** — combines Three.js particle-torus, Canvas 2D dot-rhombus, AND video hero in a single page, making it the most performance-intensive but visually stunning inspiration
- **Glassmorphism layering** — semi-transparent blurred cards floating over the dot-rhombus grid create genuine depth, not just flat sections
- **Indigo-to-cyan gradient identity** — the gradient-text headlines paired with the indigo video tint give the entire page a cohesive color story that feels unmistakably "tech"
