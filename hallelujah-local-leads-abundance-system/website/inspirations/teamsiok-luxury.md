## Component Recipe

| # | Component | Section | Notes |
|---|-----------|---------|-------|
| 1 | `sticky-blur-nav` | Global | Transparent → solid on scroll |
| 2 | `scroll-reveal` | All sections | fade-in-up |
| 3 | `hover-lift` | Service cards | Shadow deepens on hover |
| 4 | `pill-glow` | CTAs | Red solid CTAs with shadow glow |
| 5 | `counter-stats` | Stats bar | Years experience, projects completed, etc. |
| 6 | `diagonal-clip` | Section transitions | clip-path polygon for angled section edges |
| 7 | `monospace-labels` | Throughout | Category labels |
| 8 | `gold-gradient-line` | Dividers | Used as red-gradient-line with brand color |
| 9 | `marquee-ticker` | Below testimonials | Capabilities strip |

## Page Structure (top to bottom)

1. **Navigation** — `sticky-blur-nav`
2. **Hero** — Warm sunset road photo + red overlay, centered text
3. **Stats Bar** — `counter-stats` (years experience, projects completed, satisfaction rate)
4. **Services** — `hover-lift` cards with `scroll-reveal`
5. **Diagonal Clip Transition** — `diagonal-clip` (angled edge into next section)
6. **Testimonials** — `scroll-reveal` + `monospace-labels`
7. **Marquee Strip** — `marquee-ticker` (capabilities)
8. **CTA** — `pill-glow` red buttons
9. **Footer**

## Reproduction Prompt

> Build a bold, high-contrast single-page site for [CLIENT]. Sticky blur nav. Hero section with a warm photography background (construction/trade imagery) plus a red brand-color overlay and centered white text. Immediately below hero, a prominent counter-stats bar showing key numbers (years experience, projects completed, satisfaction rate) — make this prominent, construction clients love numbers. Services as hover-lift cards with deepening shadows. Use diagonal-clip (clip-path polygon) for angled transitions between sections. Testimonials section with monospace-labels. Add a marquee-ticker strip for capabilities. Dividers use gold-gradient-line adapted to the brand's red. CTAs are pill-glow with solid red and shadow. All sections scroll-reveal fade-in-up. Palette: construction. Mood: sturdy, dependable, bold. High contrast throughout.

## What Makes This One Special

- **Stats-forward layout** — the counter-stats bar sits immediately below the hero, making numbers the first thing visitors see after the headline. Construction and trade clients respond strongly to quantified credibility (years, projects, percentages)
- **Diagonal-clip transitions** — the angled section edges add visual energy and forward momentum without requiring any JavaScript, keeping the page fast on mobile devices where trade clients often browse
- **High contrast, bold CTAs** — pill-glow red buttons with shadow create unmissable conversion points. The entire page feels sturdy and dependable, matching the brand promise of a construction company
