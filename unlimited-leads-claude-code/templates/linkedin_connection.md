# LinkedIn connection requests

`write_outreach.py` generates these. This file is the formula behind them, for
when you write one by hand.

## The formula

```
[SPECIFIC THING YOU NOTICED] + [WHY YOU WANT TO CONNECT]
```

200 characters. Both halves required. Most requests have neither.

## Why most get ignored

> Hi Dana, I'd love to connect and expand my network!

Nothing specific, no reason, and identical to the forty others they got this
week. `write_outreach.py` rejects this — "building my network" and its variants
are on the banned list.

## Four that work

**Their content**
> Saw your post on why storm season kills scheduling — the point about crews
> idling between claims matched what I see. Would value staying in touch.

**Something that changed**
> Noticed Riverbend just opened in Daphne. I work with roofers on lead flow
> through expansions and would be glad to compare notes.

**Genuine admiration, specific**
> Been following how you've grown Delta Signworks into large-format work.
> Impressive run. Would like to follow along.

**Shared background**
> Fellow Tuscaloosa operator — I work with trades businesses on their pipeline.
> Thought it was worth connecting.

## Rules

- **200 characters.** Enforced in code.
- **Never pitch in the request.** The connection is the ask. Selling here gets you ignored and sometimes reported.
- **One specific detail.** If you can't name one, don't send it — go research first.
- **Say why.** "Would value comparing notes" beats no reason at all.
- **Sound like yourself.** These are read in two seconds by a human who sees a lot of them.

## After they accept

Don't pitch immediately. Wait a day, then reference the thing you mentioned:

> Thanks for connecting, Dana. How's the Daphne location settling in?

A question they can answer in one line beats a paragraph about you.

## Volume

Cap it at about 20 requests a day. Above that, LinkedIn starts restricting
accounts, and a low acceptance rate makes it worse. Specific, well-researched
requests get accepted far more often — which is the whole reason the research
step exists.
