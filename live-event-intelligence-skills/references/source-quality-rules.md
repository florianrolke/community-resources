# Source Quality Rules

Priority order:
1. Official event/association/venue page.
2. Official organizer/company post or page.
3. Industry publication preview or recap.
4. Search result snippet or public LinkedIn snippet.
5. Historical page or prior-year media.

Labels:
- `confirmed`: official/current source verifies the field.
- `probable`: strong public match, but not official enough for final outreach without review.
- `weak`: possible match only.
- `historical-reference`: useful for watchlists or sponsor patterns, not current-year confirmation.
- `needs-manual-verification`: incomplete, gated, app-only, or snippet-only evidence.

Rules:
- Do not mark a profile verified from name alone.
- Person LinkedIn matches need name plus company/title/niche evidence.
- Company LinkedIn matches need a company slug or visible company identity match.
- Event dates from official pages override third-party pages.
- Keep app-only claims separate from public-web verified data.
