# Outcome-First Opportunity Mapping

Use this reference when pasted research reports are context/training material, not tasks to execute.

## Core principle

The job is to identify rooms and relationship nodes that can create appointments, speaking slots, partnerships, sponsor visibility, or targeted prospect lists. Do not optimize for volume of findings.

## Translate research into outcomes

Ask:
- Who is the buyer or gatekeeper?
- Where do they already gather?
- Which public pages prove the room exists?
- What asset can be extracted: speakers, sponsors, exhibitors, member directory, magazine, newsletter, attendee snippets, job posts, funding signals?
- What action follows: attend, sponsor, pitch speaker slot, scrape directory, enrich organizers, or build outreach list?

## Ranking rubric

Score each opportunity 0-100:
- ICP fit (0-25): buyer/operator/founder/advisor density.
- Access quality (0-20): public organizer contacts, member directory, exhibitor/sponsor list, attendee companies, or calendar.
- Timeliness (0-15): upcoming soon, recurring, or past event with already-public lists.
- Commercial intent (0-15): attendees buy services, generate leads, hire, sponsor, or control budgets.
- Relationship leverage (0-15): association leaders, podcast hosts, chapter chairs, newsletter editors, conference organizers.
- Source confidence (0-10): official/current beats snippets and deep-research claims.

Recommended labels:
- `attend-now`: high ICP fit and upcoming.
- `sponsor-or-advertise`: newsletter/magazine/expo/listing channel exists.
- `scrape-for-leads`: member/exhibitor/sponsor/company pages are public.
- `speaker-pitch`: event has talks, panels, education, webinars, chapters, or roundtables.
- `watchlist`: strong historical pattern but no current date.
- `discard`: weak ICP fit or no actionable extraction path.

## Dealing with Perplexity/Genspark reports

Treat them as lead lists, not evidence. Verify every important event/contact against:
1. official event/association/company pages;
2. official LinkedIn/company posts;
3. industry publication previews/recaps;
4. search snippets only as weak evidence.

Do not execute every prompt in the report. Extract the durable pattern, update playbooks/query templates, then run only the pieces needed for the desired business outcome.
