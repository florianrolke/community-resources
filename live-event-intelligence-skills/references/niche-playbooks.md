# Niche Playbooks From Prior Research

These playbooks encode patterns learned from manufactured housing, AI conferences, CPA/CPE, exit planning, plumbing/HVAC, and Wichita local networking research.

## Manufactured housing / mobile homes

Preferred assets:
- state/regional manufactured housing associations;
- annual conventions, annual meetings, legislative days, installer education, park-manager training;
- official magazines/newsletters/directories;
- member/community/dealer directories;
- exhibitor and sponsor pages;
- industry publications and show calendars.

High-signal source types:
- `state association events`: IMHA, WMA, FMHA, TMHA, MHISC, PMHA, NYHA, Iowa MHA, Ohio MHA, NMMHA, MHCA/AZ.
- `industry calendars`: MHInsider, MHI, IMN, Louisville Show, Biloxi Show.
- `dealer/reseller pages`: manufacturedhomes.com dealers/floorplans and paid-listing pages.
- `community pages`: useful when they reveal marketing-active operators, but park pages alone are lower-signal than owners/operators/dealers.

Judgment:
- Investment firms are usually better found from podcasts, conference speakers, MHI/IMN events, and press releases.
- Dealers/resellers with paid listings or strong websites are often better agency prospects than raw park listings.
- Associations with magazines/newsletters are distribution channels, not just event sources.

Useful query additions:
```text
"manufactured housing" "annual convention" [year]
"manufactured housing" "member directory" [year]
"manufactured housing" "official publication"
"manufactured home communities" convention expo [year]
site:manufacturedhomes.com/dealers [city] [state]
site:manufacturedhomes.com/communities [city]
"mobile home park" podcast investor conference
```

## AI startup conferences / VapiCon-style research

Preferred assets:
- speaker pages, sponsor pages, exhibitor pages, partner pages;
- founder speaker lists;
- job pages and founder hiring posts;
- funding/news pages only when used for hiring or growth signal context.

Judgment:
- Past events are often better than upcoming events because speaker/sponsor lists are already public.
- Do not over-index on recent funding if the outcome is hiring signals; revenue traction, active careers pages, and founder hiring posts matter.
- LinkedIn/X are usually stronger than Instagram/Facebook for B2B technical founders.

Useful query additions:
```text
[event name] speakers sponsors exhibitors [year]
[event name] founder CEO CTO speaker
[company] careers hiring founder LinkedIn [year]
[company] funding [year-1] [year]
site:linkedin.com/in "[founder]" "[company]"
```

## Exit planning / transition networks

Preferred assets:
- exit planning institutes and chapter pages;
- family business centers;
- business broker and M&A associations;
- CPA/CFA/valuation groups;
- EO/Vistage/TAB-style owner peer groups;
- university family business programs;
- podcasts and webinars around exit, succession, legacy, and owner dependence.

Judgment:
- Avoid hallucinated association names. Verify each organization exists and has public chapters/events.
- The best nodes may be advisors/gatekeepers rather than business owners directly.
- Strong angle: owner pride, legacy preservation, business continuity, reducing owner dependence.

Useful query additions:
```text
"exit planning" chapter [city]
"business transition" association [state]
"family business center" succession webinar
"business broker" association chapter [city]
"Certified Exit Planning Advisor" chapter events
```

## CPA/CPE and professional education

Preferred assets:
- state society event calendars;
- foundation/education-arm pages;
- conference pages with speaker/program staff;
- topic communities and committee lists;
- staff directories for CPE, education, sponsorship, conferences.

Judgment:
- AICPA-style national organizations may not have local chapters; state societies are the practical local bridge.
- NASBA is credibility/regulatory language, not usually a local event source.
- Use precise categories: CPE, professional education, conference programming, sponsorship, technical committees.

## Local business networking / chambers / civic clubs

Preferred assets:
- chamber calendars;
- WIBA/independent business associations;
- Rotary/Lions/Kiwanis recurring lunch calendars;
- industry-specific meetups;
- ribbon cuttings, socials, luncheons, masterminds.

Judgment:
- Service clubs are not pure business networking, but recurring lunches can be high-trust local rooms.
- For local prospecting, recurring cadence often beats one-off big events.
