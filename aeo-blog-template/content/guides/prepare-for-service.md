# What should you bring to a bicycle service appointment?

Bring the bicycle and a short description of the problem, including when it occurs. Note any recent changes or repairs. Ask the workshop in advance whether it needs accessories such as a battery key, and confirm how estimates and approval for additional work are handled.

## What information helps a mechanic?

Describe what you observed and when it happens. A sound that occurs only while pedaling is a different observation from a sound while coasting. Describe the symptom without presenting your guess as a confirmed diagnosis.

## What should you confirm before leaving the bicycle?

Ask about the expected inspection scope, estimate, collection arrangements, and how the workshop will contact you. This is example content, not a description of a real business's terms.
