# How do you explain a bicycle problem to a mechanic?

Describe the symptom, when you first noticed it, and the conditions in which it occurs. Mention recent repairs or changes. If you do not know the cause, say so. Clear observations give a mechanic useful starting information without confusing a guess with an established diagnosis.

## Can a recording help?

A recording can demonstrate a sound that is difficult to reproduce. Capture it only when safe, and do not operate a device while riding. Ask the workshop how it prefers to receive supporting information.
