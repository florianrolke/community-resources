# AEO blog workflow

## Research demand before drafting

Choose a specific reader, niche, and purpose for the blog. Separate informational questions from decisions that naturally lead to your offer. Collect real questions from search results and People Also Ask, then examine the pages already answering them. Audience reviews and public discussions can reveal useful wording; do not copy other people's answers.

Map each distinct question to one main page. Group related pages under a section hub. Prefer a substantive page over many location-name substitutions with no original value.

## Use your own evidence and voice

Start from your own recordings, writings, field notes, or other material you have permission to reuse. Separate supported facts from ideas that still need checking. Never invent first-hand experience, prices, dates, qualifications, reviews, or business results. Use an actual expert review for sensitive subjects.

## Write a useful answer

Begin with a direct answer, often around 40-70 words when that suits the question. Use descriptive question headings where natural. Add relevant FAQs that answer adjacent questions, rather than repeating the headline. Include sources and useful internal links. Finish with a next step that fits the reader's intent.

Use the [article template](article-template.md). Frontmatter FAQs render visibly and feed the structured data from the same values. The format supports plain-text FAQ answers; Markdown formatting is for the main body.

## Check before publication

Build locally. Check factual support, author identity, link destinations, and schema against the visible page. Review the mobile layout. Confirm production excludes drafts and future articles. Do not change the editorial date just because a build ran.

Keep important material available as HTML text. Review robots and CDN rules deliberately: search retrieval and model training are separate choices. The generated llms.txt is an optional summary, not a ranking requirement.

## Measure and revise

Use Search Console queries and landing pages to see where impressions and clicks occur. Compare equivalent time periods and segment by device and country when relevant. Improve pages with relevant demand, weak titles, or incomplete answers. Measure inquiries and useful conversions separately from traffic or a bot-readiness score.

See [the ranking checklist](ranking-checklist.md). No traffic or conversion result is claimed by this template.
