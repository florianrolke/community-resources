# Ranking and measurement checklist

- Confirm the live domain, canonical URLs, status codes, crawl rules, and production sitemap.
- Remove unintended noindex directives only from pages approved for public search.
- Verify ownership in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools; submit the production sitemap.
- Check that HTML includes the content without requiring an agent to click or submit a form.
- Validate structured data and ensure author/business details and FAQ answers match the page.
- Link each useful guide from a hub and related articles. Avoid isolated pages and duplicate search intent.
- Examine Search Console queries and pages regularly. Investigate relevant impressions with poor clicks and weak answers before producing more pages.
- Track inquiries and conversions separately from rankings, CTR, and AI citations. A CTR alone does not establish lead generation.
- Record real publication and revision dates, the change made, and the next measurement period.
- Ask representative questions of AI tools and record citations as observations. Results vary and are not a controlled attribution test.
